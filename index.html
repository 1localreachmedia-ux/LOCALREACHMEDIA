Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can a competing business appear on the same postcard?

No. Each edition is designed around one business per industry category.

Is ad design included?

Yes. LocalReach Media creates the ad using the materials and offer supplied by the advertiser.

When is payment due?

After category availability and placement are confirmed. The spot is not reserved until payment is received.

How do premium spots fit?

Premium placements are larger and positioned beside the central LocalReach panel or in high-visibility corners. The featured ad is the largest single placement on the back.

Are the 2,746 homes guaranteed?

The campaign is currently planned around approximately 2,746 residential addresses. Final USPS route counts are confirmed before printing.
