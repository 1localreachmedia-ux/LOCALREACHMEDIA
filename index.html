Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a competing business appear on the same postcard?

No. Each edition is limited to one business per industry category.

Is design included?

Yes. LocalReach Media designs the advertisement using the materials supplied by the advertiser.

When is payment due?

Payment is due after category availability and placement are confirmed.

How do premium spots fit?

Premium placements are larger and placed beside the central LocalReach panel or in prominent corners. The featured ad is the largest single placement on the back.
